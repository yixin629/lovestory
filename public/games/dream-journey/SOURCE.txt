Source: https://github.com/liangtongzhuo/game_web

This local-practice prototype reuses the background image and eight-direction
character animation frames from the source project. The original Canvas demo
was rewritten as a typed React/Next.js feature with desktop controls, NPCs,
quests, combat, responsive rendering, and local save data.

The following battle assets are original AI-assisted project artwork generated
for this repository on 2026-09-01 and do not come from the upstream demo or any
commercial game:

- battle/arena-formation-v1.png
- battle/partners-atlas-v1.png
- battle/moon-lotus-actions-v1.webp
- battle/moon-fox-actions-v1.webp

Generation direction: original 2.5D Chinese-fantasy art, with no copied named
characters, logos, UI, or source-game assets.
